01 / 07  ·  Introduction

Single-shot calibration feasibility study for multi sensor rigs

We calibrate the intrinsics and extrinsics of 9 cameras and 8 LiDARs jointly against a known environment (a reference point cloud plus 3D AprilTag corners), in simulation, where the true answer is known. Then we change the scene (board count, board layout, rig motion) and see where the calibration converges and how accurate it is.

02 / 07  ·  Generated assets

Boards, room and sensor rigs are all generated

Tag boards

  • generate_boards.py writes USDZ plates with tag36h11 tags (300 A3, 140 large, 1 m).
  • Plate = tag·10/8: A3 = 0.30 m plate / 0.24 m tag, big = 0.9 m / 0.72 m.
  • Tag-set = 3×3 plates, no gap: 0.9×0.9 m, 9 tags on one rigid plane.

Room

  • Floor + 4 walls from room: in the config (distance, height, colour); daytime sky.
  • Sizes used: 20×20×8 m, 10×10×3 m, and a corridor room with 5 m openings in two walls.
  • Boards are placed by position + normal; a generator drops plates whose image boxes overlap another plane in any camera.

Three ways to move the rig, many ways to place boards

Spin

Rig turns in place (12 or 36 yaw steps); every camera sees the walls at many orientations. Boards on 4 walls + floor, 4–100 per face.

05 / 07  ·  Issues found

What breaks a calibration, and what fixed it

Initialisation is the cliff

Failures are almost never the bundle adjustment: Zhang's initial focal length needs ≈ 6+ tags on one plane per camera (2 tags crash calibrateCamera, 3–4 give f from 300 to 250 000 px). Minimum ≈ 50–60 boards for single shots, 25 for a spin.

Parallel planes and pure translation

A straight corridor drive shows each camera only parallel walls: f degenerates (1e5). Pooling the same plane over frames was also wrong; the init now takes views round-robin over distinct planes (init f 573–1185 → 773–838 px).

Symmetry kills the spin

Square room and 90°-symmetric rig: spins of 4, 2 or 1 positions always fail (f 2.6e5) for every board count; 3, 6, 9, 12 positions work.

Focal length vs depth

One viewpoint cannot separate f from distance along the optical axis: translation errors along the view axis, err/σ > 3. Closer boards and tilted near groups (G2) halve it; without them the large room does not converge.

Solver stability

Undamped Gauss-Newton diverged (singular matrix). Fix: Levenberg-Marquardt with reverted bad steps, cameras first, LiDARs added after. Gauge is fixed by a prior on camera 0, so σ is reported relative to it.

Simulation caveats

Overlapping boards bias detected corners; floor and ceiling tags are not seen from 2 m (floor visible only beyond ≈ 2.1 m); the reference cloud is built from the same scans, so LiDAR numbers are optimistic; Isaac Kit sometimes hangs at start-up (watchdog retries).

06 / 07  ·  Results

Best result for each modality

Single shot needs designed targets

~30 multi-tag panels (139 tags) with near tilted groups reach 2–3 mm / 0.5 px in a small room; plain boards need ≈ 50–60 and converge at 5–15 mm.

Spin is the most accurate

Minimum ≈ 25 A3 boards and 3 positions 120° apart (6 is safe); cameras within 1–6 mm, f within 0.1–0.8 px. Avoid 90° multiples.

Corridor needs rotation or panels

Best = lowest overall ranking score within the modality (mean normalised rank over 12 error metrics; 0 best, 1 worst). Errors are against simulator ground truth; err/σ ≲ 3 = inside the predicted precision. All 134 runs and per-metric charts: index.html.
