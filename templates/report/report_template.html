Single-shot Target-based Calibration, is it possible?

Joint intrinsic/extrinsic calibration (bundle adjustment: AprilTag corners + LiDAR point-to-plane against a TLS-like reference cloud) of simulated Isaac Sim captures, compared with the simulator ground truth.

Sensor rig

Layout from isaac/configs/calibration_box_tag36h11.config.yaml: sensors sit on a 10 m x 2 m box (cameras z=2.7 m, LiDARs z=1.9-2.9 m): four corner cameras looking 45deg outward, four cameras and four XT32 LiDARs looking along the box axes, a duplicate north camera 0.5 m beside camera_north, and four OS1 LiDARs at the corners. Extrinsics are reported relative to camera_corner_ne (reference camera, tied to the map by a 1e-3 prior). Initial extrinsics in all runs = ground truth. Errors: translation = |tresult - tGT|, rotation = angle of RGTT Rresult; err/σ = largest |t error| / predicted σ over the axes.

Note, experiment F: the 10 m wide box does not fit a 10x10 m room (end cameras would sit in the walls), so F uses the same rig with all x/y offsets scaled by 0.5 (5 m x 1 m box, same heights and orientations).
